<Update label="June 22, 2026">

## New features

**Kostenträger-Einsatznachweis export per child**

Admins can now export a child's logged hours in the official "Tabelle 7" layout from **Kind → Details öffnen → Historie → Exportieren**. Choose PDF, Excel, or CSV, for a single month or a month range, combined or one file per Schulbegleiter.

- Per-child **approved monthly hours** (direct and indirect) entered in the Kinder wizard and Allgemeines tab.
- Optional **"Stunden mit indirekter Leistung auffüllen"** safety net tops up the indirect row to the approved monthly budget when Schulbegleiter under-log, split fairly across assistants.
- Optional **"Unterschriften einbetten"** embeds each day's signature into the PDF.

**Holidays and Feiertage in the week view**

School holidays (Ferien) and public holidays (Feiertage) now appear in the week calendar, with a native date picker and a tidier three-dot menu for day actions.

**Schulbegleiter can report a child sick**

Schulbegleiter can now mark a child as sick from the entry sheet without having to message an admin. Reports can be cancelled if entered by mistake.

## Updates

**Reworked timesheet entry sheet**

The new entry flow is faster and clearer:

- Start and end times **auto-fill from the assigned child's Stundenplan** when entering a direct entry (still editable).
- **Indirekt** uses a free-text child-name field instead of the search combobox.
- **Vertretung** moved from a top-level type to a sub-button under Arbeit, between "Direkt" and "Indirekt".
- "Eigenes Kind" is now labelled **"Direkt"**.

**Per-child billing toggles**

Three new toggles on each child:

- **Vorviertelstunde** / **Nachviertelstunde** — bills an extra quarter hour before or after the daily direct service, applied once per day in the Kostenträger export only. Einträge, Historie and the monthly Freigabe remain unaffected.
- **Ausflüge & Schullandheim** — informational flag.

**Signatures in the Historie list**

Signed entries now show an inline signature image next to the "Unterschrieben" badge in the Historie list, so you can verify entries at a glance.

**Indirekt as real events**

Indirekte Leistung is now stored as proper event rows and feeds directly into the Kostenträger export's indirect totals.

## Bug fixes

- **Admin rename of a Schulbegleiter** now correctly syncs the displayed name and child route everywhere.
- **Stundenplan and Vertretung** behave correctly with per-day assignments; replaced Einsätze are hidden from the week calendar.
- Various small entry-sheet and admin-edit fixes carried over from earlier this week.

</Update>
