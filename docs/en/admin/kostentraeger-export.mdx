The **Einsatznachweis** is the billing document for a child's **cost bearer
(Kostenträger)** — "in the format of Tabelle 7". You generate it from the recorded work
entries of a single child.

<Tip>
  If several children are billed together via a pool, generate the Einsatznachweis for the
  entire pool directly from the [Pools](/en/admin/pools) area.
</Tip>

## Open the export

In the [**Children**](https://lebenshilfe.codingforchange.com/admin/children) area, open a
child via **Details bearbeiten** and switch to the **Historie** tab. From there, use
**Exportieren** to open the **Einsatznachweis exportieren** dialog.

## Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose period">
    Choose between **Einzelner Monat** (single month) and **Zeitraum (mehrere Monate)**
    (range). Depending on your choice, a **Monat** field or **Von** and **Bis** fields
    appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set split">
    Under **Aufteilung** (split) decide:
    - **Zusammengefasst (alle Schulbegleiter)** — one file for the child, or
    - **Pro Schulbegleiter (eine Datei je SB)** — one file per school assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose format">
    Choose **PDF**, **Excel (.xlsx)**, or **CSV**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set options">
    Enable the extra options if needed (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Exportieren**. A confirmation appears ("Einsatznachweis wurde erstellt." or the
    matching count for multiple files) and the file(s) download.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Extra options

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Fill hours with indirect service">
    A safety net: if the logged hours aren't enough, the "Indirekte Leistung" row is
    filled up to the child's approved indirect service quota.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Embed signatures (PDF only)">
    Adds the stored signature for each day as a small column in the PDF. This option is
    only available with the **PDF** format.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  If there are no entries in the selected period, "Keine Einträge im gewählten Zeitraum."
  appears. Check the period and split in that case.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  You set a child's **cost bearer** on their master data in the Children area (step
  **Verwaltung**).
</Tip>
